# UI design: INKDECK/OS screens

Design canvas (claude.ai artifact):
<https://claude.ai/artifact/CkQZ5d3cCFz6dFwQNNuwof>

News has a canvas of its own (its stories, a story, the menu, the feeds), and its off face
another: <https://claude.ai/artifact/RyfbeTdeHMuPfKeE2QeNU6>,
<https://claude.ai/artifact/KFwBiZpDwwxepbyFjPSPR4>

Comms has one too (Home with COMMS, the talk screen, its menu, the Groups sheet, the call sheet
over another screen; its artboards for listening to a room aren't built: on 2026-11-23 the open
mic, a board's mic on or off, replaced push-to-talk and that feature):
<https://claude.ai/artifact/5VV6PUfuKHJ9aswkwmYLxQ>. Rings, conversations, messages and the mic's
screens have no artboards: their sheets follow the call sheet's look, the Messages sheet the
Groups sheet's, and a board's mic screen the talk screen's.

The canvas is private to its owner. To give someone else access, use its **Share** menu. Each
screen is a separate artboard. Home and every app screen's `[BOOT]` key are linked, so you can
click through them in Play mode. Hovering a row on Home stands in for the rotary switch.

The direction is brutalist and terminal-like: a "cyberdeck" look built only from black ink on
paper.

## Orientation

| Screens | Size | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home and all app screens | **480×800 portrait** | Panel rotated 90° in software. Same 48,000-byte 1 bpp framebuffer |
| Clock, Level, pictures held in landscape, power-off screen | **800×480 landscape** | Native panel orientation turned 180° (firmware), so the rotary wheel is on the top edge. The navigator swaps ▲ and ▼ there: rolling left is ▲, right is ▼, except in the picture viewer, which turns with the board and keeps the portrait sense (`Screen::swapsDial()`) |

Artboards are drawn at 1 CSS px = 1 panel pixel, so sizes in the mockups map directly to device
pixels.

## Visual rules

- **1-bit only.** Every mark is ink or paper. The mockups use `#111111` ink on `#E8E6DF` paper so
  they look like the panel; on the device these become black and white. No opacity and no grey
  text.
- **Greys are dithers.** Fills that need a tone (progress bars, waveform not yet played, unplayed
  rain bars, the battery's partial cell) use a 2×2 checkerboard, which is 50 % ink.
- **Selection is inversion.** The cursor row is white-on-black with a `▶` marker. There's no
  hover or focus colour.
- **Structure:** 3 px rules between regions, 1 px dashed rules between rows, hard edges, no radii.
  The few "buttons" have a dithered drop shadow offset 6 px.
- **Type:** JetBrains Mono (400/500/700/800) for all UI. Literata for book text in the reader.
  Both are SIL OFL 1.1, so they can be converted to bitmap fonts for the firmware. The minimum
  size is 13 px for labels; body text is 15–19 px.
- **Icons:** stroke-only and pixel-stepped (square caps, crisp edges), drawn on a 48-unit grid.
  No emoji.

## Input mapping

The device has no touch screen. All navigation uses the buttons in `include/board_pins.h`:

| Hint | Button | GPIO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `▲` / `▼` | Rotary switch up / down (`BTN_UP`, `BTN_DOWN`); swapped on landscape screens, where the wheel is on top | 4 / 6 |
| `●` | Rotary push (`BTN_PRESS`); `HOLD ●` = long press (≈1 s) | 5 |
| `BOOT` | Back (`BTN_BOOT`): taps go back a screen at a time to Home, where a tap does a full refresh. `HOLD BOOT` (≈0.5 s) = talk to the agent from any screen (in a build with `INKDECK_AGENT`; `src/apps/hold_boot.h`): a push-to-talk chirp (three quick 1500 Hz pips, after Nextel's) says the mic is open, the agent sheet rises, and letting go sends (`src/apps/agent_sheet.h`). Over the Agent's screens the words go to the agent (a new session from the list, into the session on screen); anywhere else they are a talk turn, the limited assistant that sees no sessions (issue #52). While music or a podcast is playing (not paused), `HOLD BOOT` raises the player sheet instead (nothing on the full player). On the Comms screens and sheets it talks to the agent as anywhere (their mic has its own switch, ●; the engine pauses an open mic while the agent listens or speaks, and the screens say `PAUSED · AGENT LISTENING`), with one exception: on the dialer's ringing sheet (in a build with `INKDECK_COMMS`) it records a message for the ring nobody answered instead (from 5 s; `ui::TalkTo::message`), before the player sheet (issue #66): RECORDING with the meter while held, and letting go sends it. If it can't start, the sheet says why in a `!` notice (`A MESSAGE CAN GO FROM 0:05`). On the Comms screens and sheets, a tap of BOOT first stops PLAY LAST or a message playing (`[BOOT] STOP`) | 0 |
| `PWR` | PMIC `PWRON` key: 128 ms = on, 4 s = forced off | — |

`HOLD BOOT` neither talks nor raises the player sheet while the lock screen is on the stack or under an
off face (Sleep, Standby, a closed book), and doesn't talk over the Recorder or a ringing alarm
(`Screen::allowsTalk()`). `PWR` works as usual.

Lock screen's wheels unless it was used in the last minute (Lock above). Music (nothing playing)

has its own **Library** (the table above); without `SD:/Books`, or for a book outside it, its
LIBRARY opens Files proper at the book's folder. The card's root starts
with an `ONBOARD FLASH` row that opens the 9.9 MB FAT partition in the same browser. A held dial
moves a page at a time in any long list. The ● verb follows the selection: OPEN, READ (EPUB,
or any file that reads as text), PLAY, VIEW (JPEG, PNG). HOLD ● deletes the selection (any entry but

then CANCEL (selected) and DELETE, with the file's size or what the folder holds

- **Picture viewer** (`src/apps/viewer.h`): the picture fitted to the whole panel (at most 2×)
  and centred on paper, portrait or landscape as the board is held (the IMU). Turning the board
  turns the picture once the new pose has held for two readings a second apart (a full
  refresh); 30–60° is neither, so it doesn't flicker near 45°, and upside down keeps the
  current orientation. Only the tilt decides, not the picture's shape: lying flat, it keeps the
  current orientation (portrait if it opened flat). The dial isn't swapped in landscape here, so
  the same roll steps the same way either way. The chrome (status bar
  `11 FILES · 03/12 name.jpg`, hints `[▲] PREV [▼] NEXT [●] HIDE [BOOT] BACK`) is drawn over the
  picture's edges, and ● hides or shows it without redrawing the picture. With the chrome hidden
  and the comms mic on, a `MIC` tag on a paper margin in the top right corner stands in for the
  status bar's (issue #66). Counts as reading.

**Network shares** (`src/shares.h`, from `smb =` and `nfs =` lines in `SD:/inkdeck.ini`) follow
the card root's `ONBOARD FLASH` row, a row each: the share's name, an `SMB` or `NFS` tag, no size. Opening one pushes a
Files screen for it with a `// CONNECTING…` header and the share's name and
its URL (no password) in the body while it joins Wi-Fi and mounts the share (hints: `BOOT
BACK` only). Then it lists the share like the card: the folder name in the header (the share's
name at its root), `SMB:/NAME/path/` or `NFS:/NAME/path/` in the status bar, and the same ●
verbs. If the mount fails, the body says `CAN'T CONNECT` with the reason (`WI-FI: NETWORK NOT
FOUND`, `SIGN-IN FAILED (USER OR PASSWORD?)`, `NO SUCH SHARE`, `NO SUCH EXPORT`, `ACCESS DENIED`,
`NO ANSWER FROM THE SERVER`) and ● RETRY. Shares are read-only: no HOLD ● DELETE. In its place,
HOLD ● SAVE (on a file of a type the board opens) copies it to the card, into a top-level folder
by its type. A menu sheet `// SAVE NAME.EXT TO` lists the choices with where each lands on the
right (`SD:/Podcasts/Show/`, or `ALREADY THERE` for a file of that name and size): MP3 → MUSIC
and PODCASTS, WAV → MUSIC, JPEG/PNG → PICTURES, EPUB → BOOKS, other text → DOCUMENTS. The
cursor starts on the library the share's path names (a file under its `podcasts` folder: on
PODCASTS). Music keeps the folders below the share's `music` folder (else the album's); a
podcast goes in its show's folder (the one below the share's `podcasts` folder). While it
copies, an inverted `// SAVING NAME.EXT…` header with `42% · 3 MIN` on the right (the time
left, in tens of seconds under 90 s) replaces the folder header, and the hints show only
`BOOT STOP`. The outcome comes up as a notice: `SAVED TO SD:/PODCASTS/SHOW`, `CAN'T SAVE: CARD
FULL` (or `CONNECTION LOST`, `SD WRITE FAILED`, ...), `STOPPED; NOTHING SAVED`.
